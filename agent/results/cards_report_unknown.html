Domain card test

Which card the agentic identifier attaches to each chatbot, and whether it is the right one. Made 2026-11-11 by test_cards.py.

How a card is chosen

1. Exact versionfull card (all 4 domains)
→ if version Unknown
2. Familyfields shared by the line's known models (needs 2+ known versions)
→ if family Unknown
3. Developerfields shared by the company's models
→ if nothing
4. No cardsent for review

A card is right when the chatbot's real model belongs to it (same version, family or developer), and most specific when no more specific card was possible with the models the identifier knew. Hidden models are never used for cards.
